# Disclosure

JD Jones directed this project. Claude (Anthropic) in Claude Code was used throughout, and the repository was written in
that process: Graham's texts were read as printed pages (the 1966 paper from the Bell System Technical Journal scan and
his 1972 survey from a PDF the owner supplied; the 1969 paper itself was not reachable, and its locators rest on an
earlier reading, as formalization.yaml says), and the statements, the standard-library replay, the Lean proofs, the

proved by a counting argument in place of his exchange argument (PROOF.md). The papers are cited, not copied.

(PalomarRegistry/PalomarSubmission), copied with a three-line provenance comment; no other code is derived from another
project, and the neighbouring Lean and Isabelle work named in README.md is cited, not used. Verification status is stated
in VERIFICATION.md. No independent human review or source-author endorsement is claimed.
